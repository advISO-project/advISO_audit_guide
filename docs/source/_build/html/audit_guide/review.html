Reviewing Audits for Bioinformatics Teams

Opportunities for improvement

Acting on risks

Prevent or reduce undesired impacts of bioinformatics pipelines and workflows

Findings that inform the next risk assessment and audit schedule

Perhaps through an audit you identified that a piece of bioinformatics software is no longer supported and needs replacing.

Perhaps through an audit you identified that a database used in a bioinformatics pipeline is no longer being updated as expected.

Perhaps through an audit you identified that the quality control thresholds used in a bioinformatics pipeline are no longer appropriate for the type of data being processed.

Identifying opportunities for improvement is a key part of the audit process, and indeed a quality-focused culture.

Management review

Audits should not be seen as a punitive exercise, but rather as a way to identify areas for improvement and to ensure that bioinformatics pipelines and workflows continue to be fit for purpose, especially in a field that is rapidly evolving.
