==================================
Performing a Bioinformatics Audit
==================================
Do you have auditors are who trained and competent to assess the performance of the management system, and, whenever resources permit, are independent of the activity to be audited?

Independent and impartial 

The person who designs the audit plan, performs the audit, and reports the results should be independent of the activity being audited. This ensures that the audit is impartial and objective, and that the findings are credible and reliable.

This may not always be possible in small bioinformatics teams, where the same people may be responsible for designing, performing, and reporting on audits. In such cases, it is important to ensure that the audit process is transparent and that any potential conflicts of interest are disclosed and managed appropriately.

Low resource teams may also consider using external auditors or peer reviewers to provide an independent perspective on the audit process and findings.
