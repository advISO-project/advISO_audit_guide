Risk assessment

The focus on risk
-------------------
The ISO 15189:2022 standard places risk management at the core of the laboratory quality management system. For laboratories transitioning from wet-lab to dry-lab environments, you must consider the risks of bioinformatics processes about risk before you can build and perform an audit.
