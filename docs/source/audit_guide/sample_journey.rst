===============================================
Sample Journey
===============================================

Before designing audits, it might be useful to think about the *sample journey* through your bioinformatics process. This is an exercise which can help you identify the key steps in your process, and the associated risks and controls which should be audited. It can also help you identify where to focus your audit efforts, and what type of audit (vertical, horizontal, or cross-audit) is most appropriate for each step.

The sample journey is a conceptual representation of the path a sample takes through the bioinformatics process, from initial input to final output. It highlights the key steps, potential risks, and controls that should be considered during audits.
