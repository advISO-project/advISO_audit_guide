=====================================================
A modular alternative to performing internal audits
=====================================================

Heading 1 (Level 2)
-------------------
Defining a single audit structure that will work well for all possible applications can be challenging, as audits will have very different requirements and content compared to those descriving bioinformatics workflows, training procedures, verification and validation, or the myriad of other processes that may require auditing. Likewise, defining a universal audit template that fits all organisations is impractical due to variations in organisational structure, size, regulatory environments, and specific operational needs.

Creating a one-size-fits-all audit template would therefore lead to sections that are irrelevant or redundant for certain types of procedures, making the audit process less efficient, more time-consuming, and risky for the laboratory. 

To address these challenges, we propose a modular approach to internal audits, where the audit structure is composed of interchangeable modules that can be tailored to the specific needs of each audit.
 

How the modules fit together
-----------------------------

Rather than following one fixed audit template, this guide treats each stage of the audit process, risk assessment, scheduling, evidence-gathering, and so on, as a self-contained module. Each module covers one part of the audit process, and can be combined with others depending on what is being audited.
