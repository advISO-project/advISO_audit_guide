==============
Audit Schedule
==============

An audit schedule turns your risk assessment and sample journey into a practical audit plan. 

Under ISO 15189:2022 standards, internal audits are planned based on the level of risk associated with a process and findings from previous audits. 

High-risk processes are audited more often. An audit schedule does not need to be static. It can change in response to risks shifting, new equipment being added, or bioinformatics processes evolving.

Audit Frequency Guidelines
==========================

You can adjust these timelines to fit your bioinformatics laboratory's specific needs, but an example of mapping risk levels from a risk assessment to a schedule is outlined below:

Low Risk 🟢
   | Frequency: Annually
Medium Risk 🟡
   | Frequency: Every 6 months
High Risk 🔴
   | Frequency: Every 3 months


Process 1: Bioinformatics pipeline for variant calling that informs drug prescription decisions. This is a high-risk process, as errors could directly impact patient care.
