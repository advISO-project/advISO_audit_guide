==============
Audit Schedule
==============

An audit schedule turns your :doc:`risk assessment <risk_assessment>` into a practical action plan. Under ISO 15189:2022 standards, internal audits are planned based on the level of risk associated with a process and findings from previous audits. 

High-risk processes are audited more often. An audit schedule does not need to be static. It can change in response to risks shifting, new equipment being added, or processes evolving.
